Privacy and safety

Use non-sensitive media

Uploads are not stored by this site. A configured third-party detector still needs the image to perform analysis and may process it under its own terms. Do not upload intimate images, confidential records, private client work, government identifiers, medical material, or media you do not have permission to share.

A high confidence score can still be wrong. Screenshots, compression, edits, filters, small faces, mixed synthetic content, and new generation methods can change model performance.

Deepfake checker FAQ

Six direct answers about formats, privacy, confidence, and the limits of automated detection.

Can a deepfake checker prove that an image is fake?

No. Detection results are probabilistic signals and can be wrong. Combine them with source checks, metadata review, reverse-image search, and qualified human analysis.

Which image formats are supported?

The checker accepts JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP images up to 5 MB. Video and audio are not supported in this version.

Is my uploaded image stored?

Calculator All-in-One does not write checker uploads to disk or keep an upload history. A configured detection provider receives the image for analysis under that provider’s own processing terms.

What does the confidence score mean?

Confidence describes how strongly the configured model supports its displayed classification. It is not the probability that every factual claim about the image is true.

Why might the result be uncertain?

Compression, screenshots, edits, filters, low resolution, unfamiliar generation methods, and mixed real-and-synthetic content can weaken detectable signals.

Can I use the result as legal proof?

No. The result is not forensic certification or legal proof. High-stakes cases require preserved evidence, provenance records, and a qualified forensic professional.
