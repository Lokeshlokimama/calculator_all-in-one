What this QR generator does

The Unlimited QR Code Generator turns common sharing requests into scannable static QR codes. Choose the type you need, enter the details, preview the result, then download the code as PNG or SVG.

It supports website links, plain text, email, phone, SMS, WhatsApp, Wi-Fi access, vCard contacts, UPI payment links, map coordinates, and calendar events. The QR image is generated in your browser, so there is no daily quota and no QR image provider request for ordinary use.

How it works

Each QR request type is converted into the correct text format. For example, an email QR becomes a mailto: link, a Wi-Fi QR becomes a WIFI: record, and a contact QR becomes a vCard. That final payload is encoded into a QR matrix with error correction and rendered as an image.

Short payloads create cleaner QR codes. Long paragraphs, large contact cards, or detailed events create denser grids that need a larger print size and sharper contrast.

Supported QR request types

  • Website URL and text for links, labels, notes, menus, and instructions.
  • Email, phone, SMS, and WhatsApp for fast contact actions.
  • Wi-Fi for network name, password, security type, and hidden network setting.
  • vCard contact for name, phone, email, company, title, site, and address.
  • UPI, maps, and events for Indian payment links, meeting places, and calendar invites.

Privacy note

The QR payload is rendered locally in the browser. Calculator All-in-One does not upload the text, Wi-Fi details, contact information, or payment note to a QR image API. The page may still load normal site services such as analytics, advertising, fonts, or hosting assets, so avoid placing passwords, private recovery codes, confidential documents, or long-term secrets in any public online utility.

Example uses

For a restaurant menu, choose Website URL and enter your menu page. For a guest network sign, choose Wi-Fi and enter the SSID and password. For a business card, choose Contact card. For a small shop payment stand in India, choose UPI payment and add the UPI ID, payee name, and optional amount.

Benefits

  • Create unlimited static QR codes locally
  • Download PNG for quick sharing
  • Download SVG for crisp print designs
  • Preview the encoded payload before use
  • Keep QR input away from third-party QR image APIs

Static QR codes are simple and durable: they encode exactly what you enter. If the destination changes later, generate a fresh QR code and replace the old printed code.

Printing and scan safety

Keep a clear quiet zone around the QR code, use strong contrast, and test the final printed size with more than one phone. Avoid glossy glare, curved surfaces, heavy logo overlays, and tiny low-resolution prints.

A short URL usually scans well at small sizes. A dense vCard or event QR needs more physical space because the modules are smaller.

Limitations

This is not a dynamic QR hosting service. It does not provide scan analytics, editable redirect links, campaign tracking, expiration controls, or account-based QR history. It creates static QR files from the details you enter.

Scanner behavior depends on the phone, camera app, installed apps, and operating system. Always test UPI, WhatsApp, Wi-Fi, contact, and event QR codes on the real devices your audience will use.

Common mistakes

  • Using a very long URL with tracking parameters when a shorter clean URL would scan better.
  • Using low contrast colors, such as pale gray on white.
  • Printing the QR code too small for a dense payload.
  • Forgetting to test the final printed design before bulk printing.
  • Putting private secrets in a QR code that will be displayed publicly.

FAQs

What QR code types can I create?

You can create static QR codes for URLs, text, email, phone, SMS, WhatsApp, Wi-Fi, contact cards, UPI payments, map locations, and calendar events.

Do these QR codes expire?

No. Static QR codes do not expire by themselves, but the destination page, payment ID, phone number, or event details may become outdated.

Is my QR data uploaded?

No QR image provider receives the payload. The browser creates the QR image locally on the page.

Can I use it for business?

Yes. Use the PNG for quick sharing or SVG for print layouts, and scan-test before publishing or printing.

What should I enter for website QR codes?

Use a complete URL beginning with https://. The tool will add https:// for normal domain names, but complete links are best.

Why test before printing?

Small size, low contrast, damaged printing, glossy surfaces, and dense data can make scanning fail.
