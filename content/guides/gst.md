## Reconcile an inclusive price correctly

### Adding and removing are different operations

Take a hypothetical base price of 1,000 and a selected tax rate of 18%. Adding tax gives 180 of tax and a total of 1,180. To recover the base from 1,180, divide by 1.18. Subtracting 18% of the inclusive total would subtract 212.40 and leave 967.60, which is wrong. The difference occurs because the rate applies to the base, not to the larger tax-inclusive amount.

This example demonstrates the selected rate's arithmetic. It does not say that 18% applies to your product or service. Classification, the applicable notification, exemptions, and transaction circumstances must be determined independently. The calculator accepts a rate; it does not make that legal determination for you.

### Keep the calculation attached to its invoice line

If an invoice contains two items with different applicable rates, calculate each taxable line using its own base and rate before adding totals. A blended rate entered against the whole invoice can hide which line produced the tax. Discounts and the basis on which they apply also matter: confirm the taxable value from the invoice rather than assuming every discount changes that value in the same way.

For a simple arithmetic check, a 10% reduction on a hypothetical 1,000 base leaves 900. Applying an illustrative 18% tax to that reduced base gives 162 and a total of 1,062. Applying the discount after tax asks a different calculation question. Keep your order of operations explicit when explaining a discrepancy.

### Know what the result does not establish

The tax number here is not a compliant invoice by itself. Supplier and customer details, tax identifiers, invoice numbering, descriptions, and other required particulars need separate attention. The tool also does not decide whether a transaction calls for integrated tax or a split of central and state tax. Consult the official rules and a qualified tax adviser where the classification is uncertain. Use the result to check arithmetic once those inputs have been established.
