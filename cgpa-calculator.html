What this calculator does

For each subject, enter its credits and the grade point you earned on a 10-point scale. Add as many subjects as you need. The calculator returns the credit-weighted average, which is your SGPA for one semester, or your CGPA if you enter all subjects across semesters.

Formula used

GPA = Σ(Credits × Grade points) ÷ ΣCredits

A subject with more credits counts for more. Rows left completely empty are ignored.

Worked example

A semester with four subjects:

  • 4 credits, grade point 9 → 36
  • 3 credits, grade point 8 → 24
  • 2 credits, grade point 10 → 20
  • 1 credit, grade point 7 → 7

Total points 87 over 10 credits gives an SGPA of 8.70. A simple average of the four grades would be 8.50, which shows why credit weighting matters.

SGPA vs CGPA

SGPA covers one semester. CGPA covers all semesters so far. The correct way to get CGPA is to weight each semester by its total credits, not to average the SGPAs, because semesters can have different credit loads. If every semester has the same number of credits, the two methods give the same answer.

Converting CGPA to percentage

There is no single conversion; each board or university publishes its own. CBSE used CGPA × 9.5 for Class 10 when it graded that way. Many universities use CGPA × 10, and some use other formulas such as (CGPA − 0.75) × 10 or (CGPA − 0.5) × 10. Use the formula on your university’s official grade sheet or regulations, especially for job and admission applications.

Understanding each input

  • Credits — from your syllabus or grade card. Labs and projects often have fewer credits than theory papers.
  • Grade point — 0 to 10, from your university’s letter-grade table (for example O = 10, A+ = 9, A = 8 at many institutions).

Common mistakes

  • Entering the letter grade’s marks instead of its grade point.
  • Averaging SGPAs across semesters with different credits.
  • Including audit or non-credit courses, which usually carry zero credits.

FAQs

What is the difference between SGPA and CGPA?

SGPA is for one semester; CGPA is the credit-weighted average across all semesters so far.

How do I convert CGPA to percentage?

Use your university’s official formula. Common ones are CGPA × 9.5 or CGPA × 10.

Does it work on a 4-point scale?

No. Grade points must be from 0 to 10.

What if I failed a subject?

Enter its grade point (often 0) and credits as your university counts it; rules on re-attempts vary.

Can I add more subjects?

Yes. Use the add subject button for each extra row.
