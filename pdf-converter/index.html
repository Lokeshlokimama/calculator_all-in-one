How the converter works

The tool uses browser rendering and text extraction. That makes it quick and private, but it also means output quality depends on the PDF itself.

1.0

Validate the PDF

The browser checks that the selected file is a PDF under 25 MB and shows a first-page preview when the PDF can be read.

2.0

Choose an output

Word export extracts selectable text. Image export renders visible pages to canvas and downloads image files.

3.0

Review before use

Check page order, text flow, symbols, tables, and resolution. Use professional software for contracts, forms, or exact layout recovery.

Privacy and safety note

Browser processing

The selected PDF is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded to Calculator All-in-One. Still avoid sensitive contracts, medical records, bank statements, identity documents, passwords, or permission-restricted files in any general online utility. The PDF.js and ZIP libraries are loaded from a public CDN so the converter can run without installing software.

PDF converter FAQs

Short answers before you trust a converted file.

Does the PDF converter upload my file?

No. The selected PDF is read by your browser and is not sent to Calculator All-in-One servers.

Is PDF to Word layout-perfect?

No. It extracts selectable text into a Word-compatible `.doc` file. Complex layouts, columns, forms, and scanned pages may need manual cleanup.

Which image formats can I export?

You can export PDF pages as PNG, JPEG, or WebP. PNG is best for text, JPEG is widely compatible, and WebP often creates smaller files.

Can scanned PDFs become editable Word text?

Not unless the PDF already contains hidden selectable text. Image-only scans require OCR, which is a separate tool.

Why does image conversion stop at 30 pages?

Rendering many high-resolution pages can freeze a phone or low-memory laptop. The cap keeps the browser responsive and predictable.

What should I check after conversion?

Open the output and verify page order, text flow, symbols, table alignment, image quality, and missing scanned content before using it.

A useful converter should be honest about what it can preserve.
