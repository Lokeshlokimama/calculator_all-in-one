What this tool does

Choose the country code, type the phone number and, optionally, a message. The tool builds a WhatsApp click-to-chat link. Anyone who taps it on a phone or computer with WhatsApp opens a chat with that number, with your message ready to send.

Link format

https://wa.me/<country code><number>?text=<message>

The number is written in full international format with no plus sign, spaces, dashes or leading zero. The message is URL-encoded, so spaces become %20.

Worked example

Country code +91, number 98765 43210, message “Hi, I would like to book a table” produces:

https://wa.me/919876543210?text=Hi%2C%20I%20would%20like%20to%20book%20a%20table

Where to use it

  • A “Chat on WhatsApp” button on your website or Google Business Profile.
  • Instagram or YouTube bio links.
  • Printed on menus, flyers and visiting cards as a QR code; the WhatsApp QR generator page can do this directly.
  • Pre-filled messages for specific products, so you know what a customer is asking about.

Things to check

  • Do not include a leading 0 or the + sign; the tool strips anything that is not a digit from the phone number.
  • Test the link on your own phone before sharing it.
  • The number must be registered on WhatsApp. A WhatsApp Business account is a good idea for shops.
  • Country codes in the menu are limited to India, US, UK, Australia and UAE. For others, use any option and edit the digits in the generated link.

Privacy

The link is built in your browser. Remember that anyone you share the link with can see the phone number inside it.

FAQs

Do I need to save the number to use the link?

No. That is the point of a click-to-chat link.

Why does my link say the number is invalid?

Check that you did not add a leading 0, and that the number has WhatsApp.

Can I add emojis to the message?

Yes. They are URL-encoded automatically.

Is this an official WhatsApp tool?

No, but it uses WhatsApp’s published wa.me link format.

Can I make a QR code from the link?

Yes, with the WhatsApp QR code generator.
