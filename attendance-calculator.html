What this calculator does

Enter the number of classes held so far, how many you attended, and your target percentage. If you are below the target, it tells you how many consecutive classes you must attend to reach it. If you are above, it tells you how many you could miss and still stay at or above the target.

Formulas used

Current % = Attended ÷ Total × 100

Classes needed (if below target) = (Target × Total − 100 × Attended) ÷ (100 − Target), rounded up

Classes you can miss (if above target) = Attended × 100 ÷ Target − Total, rounded down

Worked examples

  • Below target: 30 attended out of 45 is 66.7%. To reach 75%: (75 × 45 − 3,000) ÷ 25 = 15 more classes. Check: 45 ÷ 60 = 75%.
  • Above target: 54 out of 60 is 90%. You could miss 54 × 100 ÷ 75 − 60 = 12 classes and still be at 54 ÷ 72 = 75%.
  • Exactly at target: 30 out of 40 is 75%; missing even one class drops you below.

Why the 75% rule matters

Many Indian universities and boards require 75% attendance to sit end-semester exams, with some allowing condonation down to about 65% for medical or other valid reasons and a fee. Rules vary by institution, and some count attendance per subject rather than overall. Check your college’s regulations, and use this tool per subject if that is how yours works.

Plan ahead, not just the next class

The numbers assume every future class happens and you attend (or miss) all of them in a row. In reality the total keeps growing. Re-run the calculation every couple of weeks with updated numbers, and keep a buffer above the minimum for illness or emergencies.

Common mistakes

  • Using overall attendance when your college counts each subject separately.
  • Forgetting labs or tutorials that count as separate classes.
  • Planning to sit exactly at 75% with no margin.

FAQs

How many classes do I need to attend to get 75%?

Enter your totals; the tool solves (attended + x) ÷ (total + x) = 75% for x.

Can I reach 100% after missing a class?

No. Once you have missed one, attending more brings you closer but never exactly to 100%.

Does it work per subject?

Yes. Enter that subject’s numbers.

What is condonation?

Many institutions allow students slightly below 75% to appear for exams for valid reasons, often with documentation and a fee.

Why is the result rounded?

Classes are whole numbers, so needed classes round up and missable classes round down, keeping you at or above the target.
