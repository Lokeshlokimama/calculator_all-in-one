# Owner requirements

Before every website task, read WEBSITE-QA-CHECKLIST.md. It is the only active checklist. Consolidate every new owner concern into that file; do not create or maintain another checklist.

UI/UX requirements apply to every public content/error page. An all-page verification must cover every route in scripts/site-files.json and every internal link/anchor, and must record browser evidence at 320, 390 and 1440 px. Historical reports are evidence only, not a replacement checklist. Never mark untested checks passed.

A request to push this website means publish tested changes to main and wait for the GitHub Pages deployment and live file/commit verification. Do not stop at a feature-branch push. Preserve unrelated local files and do not force-push.

Follow the owner's free Render plan and exclude AI PDF features. Use synthetic files for QA. Keep generated screenshots, browser records, temporary files and private uploads out of the public release.
