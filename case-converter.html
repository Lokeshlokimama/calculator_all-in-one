Convert your text

What this tool does

Type or paste text, then choose UPPER, lower, Title Case or camelCase. The converted text appears below and can be copied with one click. Conversion happens in your browser.

What each option does

  • UPPER: every letter capitalised. “hello world” → HELLO WORLD
  • lower: every letter small. → hello world
  • Title Case: the first letter of every word capitalised, the rest lowercase. → Hello World
  • camelCase: words joined with no spaces, each word after the first capitalised. “user first name” → userFirstName

Notes on Title Case

This tool capitalises every word, including short words such as “of” and “the”. Style guides often keep those lowercase in headlines (“The Lord of the Rings”). It also lowercases everything else first, so acronyms like GST or NASA become Gst and Nasa; fix those by hand after converting.

Common uses

  • Fixing text accidentally typed with Caps Lock on.
  • Tidying headings and product names.
  • Creating variable names in code with camelCase.
  • Making a list of names consistent before importing into a spreadsheet.

Limitations

Case conversion applies to scripts with upper and lower case, such as the Latin alphabet. Scripts without case, such as Devanagari or Tamil, are unchanged. Sentence case (capitalising only the first word) is not offered.

FAQs

How do I undo Caps Lock text?

Paste it and choose lower, or Title Case for names.

Does Title Case handle small words?

No. Every word is capitalised; adjust short words by hand if your style guide requires it.

What is camelCase used for?

Naming variables and functions in many programming languages.

Does it keep acronyms?

No. Title Case lowercases the rest of each word, so re-capitalise acronyms after converting.

Is my text uploaded?

No.
