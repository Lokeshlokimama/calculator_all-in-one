What this calculator does

Enter the UPS battery capacity, the battery voltage, the load you connect, and the UPS efficiency. The calculator estimates runtime in minutes (and hours) at that load.

Formula used

Runtime (minutes) = Battery Ah × Voltage × Efficiency ÷ Load (W) × 60

Worked example

A typical 600 VA desktop UPS has one 12 V, 7 Ah battery. Running a 100 W load (a desktop PC and monitor at light use) at 80% efficiency:

  • Runtime = 7 × 12 × 0.80 ÷ 100 × 60 ≈ 40 minutes on paper

In practice small sealed batteries deliver well under their rated capacity at this discharge rate, and an ageing battery even less. Expect closer to 10–20 minutes.

What a UPS is really for

A desktop UPS is designed to ride through short outages and give you time to save work and shut down cleanly, not to run a computer for hours. If you need long runtime, look at an inverter with a larger battery, or a UPS that supports external battery packs.

For servers and NAS boxes, configure automatic shutdown through the UPS’s USB or network port so the system closes safely before the battery is exhausted.

Measuring your load

The wattage on a PC power supply is its maximum, not what it draws. A typical office desktop with monitor draws 60–150 W; a gaming PC under load can draw 300–600 W. Many UPS units show the current load on their display or software, which is the best number to use. A plug-in energy meter also works.

Limitations

Battery runtime is not linear: higher loads drain sealed lead-acid batteries disproportionately faster, and capacity falls with age, typically needing replacement every 2–4 years. Manufacturer runtime charts for your exact model are more accurate than this formula.

Common mistakes

  • Using the UPS VA rating as the load.
  • Expecting a 600 VA UPS to run a PC for an hour.
  • Plugging laser printers or heaters into the UPS battery outlets.

FAQs

How long will a 600VA UPS last?

Usually 10–20 minutes for a typical office PC, depending on load and battery health.

Why is my real backup shorter than the result?

Small batteries deliver less than their rating at high discharge rates, and capacity drops with age.

Can I connect a laser printer to a UPS?

Not to the battery outlets; laser printers draw large surges when heating.

How often should I replace the UPS battery?

Typically every 2–4 years, sooner in hot rooms.

Is VA the same as watts for a UPS?

No. A UPS’s watt rating is usually about 60% of its VA rating.
