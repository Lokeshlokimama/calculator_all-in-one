Review before saving

For Office files, download the PDF directly; do not reprint the preview. If the embedded PDF preview is unavailable on your device, open the download to review it. For images and text, use Print / Save as PDF. Check the output before sharing.

What is preserved?

Office files are rendered to PDF from the original document, rather than rebuilt from extracted text. The conversion attempts to preserve formatting, tables, images, headers, footers and links. Missing fonts, unsupported features and differences in document rendering can still affect pagination and appearance. This service cannot certify an exact 1:1 match.

If every word, font, table, image and page break must match, use Export to PDF in the original Word, Excel or PowerPoint application with the original fonts installed. The converter does not rewrite your text. Images and plain text use browser printing; HTML is converted to plain text with styling and external images discarded. Legacy DOC, XLS and PPT and encrypted files are not supported on this page.

How to convert your document

  1. Choose the original file. For Office files, read the conversion limits and confirm upload permission.
  2. Select Convert to PDF for Office documents, or Prepare preview for images and text.
  3. For Office files, select Download PDF to save the actual converted document. For images and text, select Print / Save as PDF.
  4. Open the PDF and compare page count, layout, fonts, tables, images and links with the original.

For image bundles with direct PDF download, use Images to PDF. To combine existing PDFs, use Merge PDF. To extract text or images from a PDF, open PDF Converter.

Privacy and file handling

Office documents are uploaded only after you confirm permission and select Convert to PDF. They are processed in temporary storage and deleted after processing. Images and text are processed locally in your browser. Clear removes the result and temporary preview URLs. Free hosting may take about a minute to wake up.

Common questions

Will the PDF match exactly?

Exact matching is not guaranteed. The service renders the original Office document, but fonts and application-specific features may differ. Use the original application's PDF export for strict layout fidelity.

What if conversion fails?

The page reports the error instead of replacing your document with extracted text. Retry later or export from the original application.

Can I convert a scanned image?

Yes, but it stays an image. This converter does not recognize or add searchable text.
