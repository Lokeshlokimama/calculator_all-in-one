Calculator inputs

Most calculator arithmetic is processed locally in your browser memory. Calculator All-in-One does not maintain a server-side history of the personal numbers, financial amounts or measurements entered into ordinary calculators, or sell those inputs. No account is required for normal use. Some tools request external data as explained below; this policy does not imply that every operation stays on your device. Do not enter confidential financial, medical, identity or authentication information into public tools.

PDF converter files

The PDF Converter and related PDF tools read selected PDFs or images in your browser for conversion, merge, split, raster compression, password-planning notes, or OCR text extraction. Calculator All-in-One does not receive, upload, store, or keep a history of these files. The tools load public browser libraries for PDF rendering, PDF editing, ZIP packaging, PDF generation, and OCR, so avoid sensitive contracts, medical records, identity documents, bank statements, passwords, or permission-restricted files in any general online utility.

Inputs sent to third parties

The currency converter sends the selected base currency to open.er-api.com to request indicative rates. When a food name is not in the local database, the nutrition tool may send that name to USDA FoodData Central (api.nal.usda.gov) to look for a match. Do not include personal details in a food search. The QR generator formats and renders QR codes in your browser; Calculator All-in-One does not send the QR payload to a QR image provider. Wi-Fi QR codes contain the entered network password in readable form. Anyone with the QR image can recover it; use a guest network. UPI QR codes contain payment routing details, not your PIN or OTP. Never encode private account passwords, authentication tokens or records.

Google Analytics

As of 5 October 2026, this release does not load Google Analytics. Earlier versions used it to measure visits. Removing the loader stops new collection by this site; it does not delete information previously held by Google. We will update this policy and implement appropriate privacy choices before enabling optional analytics again.

Opting out of personalization does not necessarily remove advertisements or all advertising cookies. See Google advertising privacy information for further details.

Contact messages

If you email us, we receive the address and content you choose to send. We use it to respond, reproduce a reported problem, consider a correction, and improve the site. Do not email passwords, financial-account details, government identifiers, medical records, or other sensitive data.

External links and retention

Hosting providers may record IP addresses, browser information, referring pages and request timestamps for security, delivery and fault diagnosis. We do not claim these logs are anonymized: their handling and retention depend on the provider. External websites, advertising providers and email services have their own privacy and retention policies.

We do not maintain user accounts or a server-side history of ordinary calculator inputs. Disabling an analytics loader does not erase historical data held by an analytics provider. Review the provider privacy notices when using external links or services.

Children and professional decisions

The site is a general-audience utility and is not intended to collect personal information from children. Health, finance, tax, legal, and engineering outputs are educational estimates; users should verify important decisions with qualified professionals and current official sources.

Browser storage

Updated 23 September 2026: the toolbox uses local browser storage for preferences, favorite and recently used tool lists, support-message dismissal, and cached exchange rates. A session counter records tool use within a browsing session. These features are separate from third-party analytics; a recently used tool list is not a saved history of entered financial or health values. You can clear this site's data in your browser settings, which also resets these preferences and caches.

Automatic display currency

General calculators estimate a default currency using your browser time zone, then an explicit browser language region if needed. This happens locally: we do not request GPS permission or send these settings to a location service. Saved currency choices take priority. This estimate may be wrong when travelling or using custom device settings; you can override it. Currency labels do not convert entered amounts. India-specific retirement tools remain in INR.
