What this tool does

Choose a date and time, the zone it is in, and the zone you want. The converter shows the matching local date and time, including the day of the week, so you can see when a call falls on a different date.

Zones available

UTC, India (IST, UTC+5:30), New York, Los Angeles, London, Paris, Sydney and Tokyo. The converter uses each city’s official time zone rules, so daylight saving time is applied on the correct dates rather than using a fixed offset.

Worked examples

  • 10:00 AM IST on Monday 16 November 2026 is 11:30 PM on Sunday 15 November in New York (EST, UTC−5) and 4:30 AM in London (GMT).
  • The same 10:00 AM IST on 16 July 2026 is 12:30 AM in New York and 5:30 AM in London, because both are on summer time (EDT, BST).

India does not use daylight saving time, so the gap between India and the US or UK changes by an hour twice a year.

Scheduling tips

  • Always check the date, not just the time; calls between India and the Americas often cross midnight.
  • The US and Europe change clocks on different dates in March and around October–November, so the gap can be unusual for a week or two.
  • Write meeting times with the zone, such as “10:00 IST / 23:30 EST”.

Limitations

Only the listed zones are available. Results depend on your browser’s time zone data, which updates with the browser.

FAQs

How many hours ahead is IST of EST?

10 hours 30 minutes in winter (EST), and 9 hours 30 minutes when New York is on daylight time (EDT).

Does India have daylight saving time?

No. IST is UTC+5:30 all year.

Is GMT the same as UTC?

For everyday purposes, yes. London uses GMT in winter and BST (UTC+1) in summer.

Does it handle daylight saving?

Yes, using the official time zone rules for each city.

Can I add other cities?

Not currently; choose the zone with the same rules if one matches.
