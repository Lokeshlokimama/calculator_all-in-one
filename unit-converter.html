What this tool does

Choose length or weight, type a value, and pick the units to convert from and to. The result updates immediately and is shown to up to five decimal places.

Conversion factors used

Length, per metre: 0.001 km, 3.28084 ft, 0.000621371 mi.

Weight, per kilogram: 1,000 g, 2.20462 lb, 35.274 oz.

Values are converted into the base unit first, then into the target unit.

Worked examples

  • 10 m = 32.8084 ft
  • 5 mi = 8.04672 km
  • 70 kg = 154.3234 lb

Handy conversions to remember

  • 1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly; 1 foot = 30.48 cm.
  • A height of 5 ft 7 in is 67 × 2.54 = 170.18 cm.
  • 1 mile ≈ 1.609 km; 1 km ≈ 0.621 mi.
  • 1 kg ≈ 2.2 lb; 1 lb ≈ 454 g.

Indian property is often measured in square feet; this tool converts lengths, not areas. Square the length factor for areas: 1 m² = 10.764 ft².

Limitations

Only length (m, km, ft, mi) and weight (kg, g, lb, oz) are supported. For inches, convert to feet by dividing by 12. For temperature, volume or area, convert by hand with the factors above or another tool.

Common mistakes

  • Converting area with a length factor.
  • Treating 5.7 ft as 5 ft 7 in; 5.7 ft is 5 ft 8.4 in.

FAQs

How many feet are in a metre?

About 3.28084.

How do I convert feet and inches to cm?

Total inches × 2.54. For 5 ft 7 in: 67 × 2.54 = 170.18 cm.

Does it convert temperature?

No. Only length and weight.

How precise are the results?

Shown to up to five decimal places.

How many pounds is a kilogram?

About 2.20462.
