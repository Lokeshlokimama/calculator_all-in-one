What this calculator does

Enter your mileage in any of the supported units. The calculator converts it into km per unit, units per 100 km and, for liquid fuels, US and UK miles per gallon. Add a trip distance and the fuel price to see how much fuel the trip needs, its cost, and the cost per kilometre. For electric vehicles, adding the battery size also estimates full-battery range.

Formulas used

L/100 km = 100 ÷ km/L

US MPG = km/L × 2.35215; UK MPG = km/L × 2.82481

Fuel needed = Distance ÷ Efficiency

Trip cost = Fuel needed × Price

Worked example

A petrol car that gives 18 km/L, on a 100 km trip with petrol at 104 per litre:

  • Fuel needed = 100 ÷ 18 = 5.56 L
  • Trip cost = 5.56 × 104 = 577.78
  • Cost per km = 5.78
  • 18 km/L is also 5.56 L/100 km, 42.34 US MPG or 50.85 UK MPG.

Real mileage vs claimed mileage

Manufacturer figures in India are ARAI-certified under test conditions, and real-world mileage is commonly 15–30% lower, especially in city traffic with AC on. For trip planning, use your own figure: fill the tank, reset the trip meter, drive normally, refill, and divide kilometres by litres added.

Comparing fuels

Use cost per kilometre to compare: a CNG car in km/kg at the CNG price per kg, or an EV in km/kWh at your electricity rate, against petrol or diesel per km. Remember differences in purchase price, maintenance and, for EVs, charging losses.

Common mistakes

  • Using the brochure mileage for budgeting.
  • Mixing US and UK gallons; a UK gallon is about 20% larger.
  • Forgetting tolls and parking, which this calculator does not include.

FAQs

How do I calculate fuel cost for a trip?

Divide distance by mileage to get litres, then multiply by the fuel price.

How do I convert km/L to L/100 km?

Divide 100 by the km/L figure.

Does it work for CNG and EVs?

Yes. Choose the fuel type and use km/kg for CNG or LPG, and km/kWh or kWh/100 km for EVs.

Why is my real mileage lower than ARAI?

Test cycles are gentler than real traffic, AC use and loads.

Does it include tolls?

No, only fuel or charging cost.
