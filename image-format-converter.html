Convert an image

What this tool does

Choose an image from your device and the format you want, then convert. The image is drawn onto a canvas in your browser and saved in the new format, which downloads straight away. The file never leaves your device.

Which format to choose

  • JPEG: best for photos. Small files, no transparency. Saved at 90% quality here.
  • PNG: best for screenshots, logos and graphics with sharp edges or text. Supports transparency. Larger files for photos.
  • WebP: modern format for the web; usually smaller than JPEG or PNG at similar quality, and supports transparency.

Things to know

  • Transparency: converting a transparent PNG to JPEG fills transparent areas, usually with black. Use PNG or WebP to keep transparency.
  • Quality: JPEG and WebP are lossy. Converting a JPEG to PNG will not improve its quality; it only makes the file bigger.
  • Metadata: canvas conversion drops EXIF data such as camera details and GPS location, which can be useful for privacy before sharing a photo.
  • Size: the image keeps its pixel dimensions. To resize, use the aspect ratio calculator to work out the size, then an image editor.

Common uses

  • Converting WebP images saved from websites to JPEG for apps that do not accept WebP.
  • Making a smaller JPEG from a large PNG photo for email or forms.
  • Converting a screenshot to PNG for a sharp result.

Limitations

HEIC photos from iPhones open only in browsers that support HEIC. Animated GIF or WebP files are converted as a single frame. Very large images may be slow on older phones.

FAQs

Is my image uploaded?

No. Conversion happens entirely in your browser.

Why did my transparent background turn black?

JPEG does not support transparency. Choose PNG or WebP instead.

Does converting improve quality?

No. Conversion cannot restore detail lost in an earlier compression.

Can I convert HEIC?

Only if your browser can open HEIC images.

Does it remove location data?

Yes, EXIF metadata including GPS is not carried into the converted file.
