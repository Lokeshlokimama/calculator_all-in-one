What this calculator does

Enter the radius of a circle in any unit. The calculator returns the area (in square units) and the circumference, which is the distance around the edge (in the same unit as the radius).

Formulas used

Area = π × r²

Circumference = 2 × π × r

π (pi) is about 3.14159. The calculator uses full precision and rounds the answer to two decimal places.

Worked example

A circle with radius 7 cm:

  • Area = π × 49 = 153.94 cm²
  • Circumference = 2 × π × 7 = 43.98 cm

With the school approximation π = 22/7, the area is exactly 154 cm² and the circumference 44 cm, which is why 7 is a popular textbook radius.

Starting from the diameter or circumference

  • If you know the diameter, halve it: radius = diameter ÷ 2. A 30 cm pizza has radius 15 cm and area about 707 cm².
  • If you know the circumference, divide by 2π: radius = C ÷ 6.2832. This is handy for tree trunks or pipes measured with a tape.

Practical uses

  • Comparing pizza sizes: a 30 cm pizza has 2.25 times the area of a 20 cm one, not 1.5 times.
  • Estimating paint or tiles for a round table or floor area.
  • Finding the length of edging or fencing around a circular bed.

Common mistakes

  • Entering the diameter as the radius, which makes the area four times too big.
  • Giving area in plain units instead of square units.
  • Mixing units, such as a radius in cm and expecting an area in m². 1 m² = 10,000 cm².

FAQs

What is the formula for the area of a circle?

Area = π × radius².

How do I find the radius from the diameter?

Divide the diameter by 2.

What units does it use?

Calculate the full area and divide by 2.
