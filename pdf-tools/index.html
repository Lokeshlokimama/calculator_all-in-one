Choose the right processing method

Browser editing is useful for page changes and visible marks. Office conversion through LibreOffice preserves more layout than text-based preview, but unusual fonts and complex layouts still require review. PDF-to-Office exports extract text and do not recreate original tables, charts or page designs.

Repair is an attempt, not a promise to recover every damaged document. PDF/A output needs an independent validator before archival use. Text comparison excludes graphics and formatting. Typed signatures are not digital certificates. Cropping and covering text are not secure redaction.

Before submitting or sharing

Keep your original, review every changed page, verify encryption by reopening the file, and search redacted output for confidential text. Use tools only with permission. No AI summarization or translation is included.
