What this calculator does

Enter the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP) and your usual cycle length. The calculator estimates your due date, an approximate conception date, how many weeks and days pregnant you are today, and your current trimester.

How it is calculated

Due date = LMP + 280 days + (cycle length − 28)

Approximate conception = LMP + (cycle length − 14) days

This is Naegele’s rule, which assumes a 40-week pregnancy counted from the start of the last period, with an adjustment for cycles longer or shorter than 28 days. Pregnancy weeks are counted from the LMP, so you are considered about 2 weeks pregnant at conception.

Worked examples

  • LMP 1 January 2026, 28-day cycle: due date 8 October 2026, conception around 15 January.
  • Same LMP, 32-day cycle: due date 12 October 2026, conception around 19 January.

How reliable is a due date?

A due date is an estimate. Only a small share of babies arrive on the exact day; most are born within about two weeks either side. An early ultrasound, ideally in the first trimester, is usually more accurate than LMP dating, especially if your cycles are irregular or you are unsure of the date. If your scan gives a different date, your doctor or midwife will normally use the scan date.

Trimesters

  • First trimester: weeks 1 to 12
  • Second trimester: weeks 13 to 27
  • Third trimester: week 28 until birth

Limitations

The calculation does not suit pregnancies after IVF or other fertility treatment, where the transfer date gives a more accurate estimate, or very irregular cycles. Cycle lengths from 20 to 45 days are accepted. This tool gives general information only; antenatal care with a qualified doctor or midwife is important throughout pregnancy.

FAQs

How is the due date calculated?

By adding 280 days to the first day of your last period, adjusted for cycles that are not 28 days long.

Why does pregnancy count from my last period, not conception?

The LMP is usually known, while conception is not. By convention, pregnancy is dated from the LMP.

Is an ultrasound due date more accurate?

Usually yes, especially an early scan. Doctors often update the due date after the first-trimester scan.

Can I use this after IVF?

Not accurately. Your clinic will calculate the due date from the embryo transfer date.

Is my data stored?

No. The dates are used only in your browser to show the result.
