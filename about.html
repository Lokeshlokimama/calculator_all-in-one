Our mission and methodology

Calculator All-in-One is an independent collection of calculation utilities, financial modeling tools and everyday converters. We make common calculations accessible without requiring an account, and explain the assumptions so readers can check the result.

Most arithmetic runs in your browser. Currency conversion requests exchange rates from an external service, and nutrition searches may query USDA FoodData Central. Hosting, fonts, browser libraries and advertising also involve third parties. The Privacy Policy explains these requests; we do not claim that every interaction stays on your device.

Formula verification and accuracy

Financial tools use compound-interest and reducing-balance amortization models. Loan guides show worked examples and explain the timing of payments. Actual lender quotes may include fees, rounding conventions and changing rates that the model does not capture.

Health tools use the formulas identified on their pages, including BMI and Mifflin–St Jeor energy estimates. These are population-based estimates and cannot diagnose a condition or replace individual clinical advice. Math and engineering tools state units and conversion factors; tariff and pension tools identify regional assumptions rather than suggesting a universal rule.

Editorial methods and sources

Guides explain inputs, units, formulas, worked examples and common mistakes. Where rules or health interpretations matter, pages link to relevant reference sources. The Editorial Standards describe sourcing, corrections and the distinction between a tested calculation and professional advice.

Automated checks exercise published examples and invalid inputs, and validate navigation, metadata and routes. Passing tests supports the implementation; it does not certify every real-world situation. We revise a formula, example or instruction when a reproducible issue is found.

Maintenance and corrections

Calculator All-in-One maintains the tools and their explanations under the site brand. We do not represent the site as a bank, government agency, medical practice or accredited advisory service.

For a formula correction, include the page address, input values, displayed result, expected result and the source you used. Send feature requests and accessibility issues through the Contact page or contact@calculatorsallinone.com. Do not send private financial records, passwords or medical documents.

Funding and independence

The core tools are free to use. Advertising may help support hosting and maintenance. AdSense connection code is installed; ad serving depends on Google approval and account settings. Advertisements must remain separate from calculator controls and must not influence formulas or results. Using a calculator never requires clicking an advertisement.
