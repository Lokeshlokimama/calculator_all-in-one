What this calculator does

Enter two durations as hours and minutes, choose add or subtract, and the calculator returns the result in hours and minutes. Minutes must be 0 to 59. Blank boxes count as zero. A negative result is shown with a minus sign.

How it works

Total minutes = hours × 60 + minutes, for each duration. The minutes are added or subtracted, then converted back: hours = total ÷ 60 (whole part), minutes = the remainder.

Worked examples

  • 2h 30m + 1h 45m = 150 + 105 = 255 minutes = 4h 15m
  • 8h 00m − 0h 45m (lunch) = 7h 15m worked
  • 1h 20m − 2h 00m = −0h 40m

Decimal hours for timesheets and pay

Payroll often needs decimal hours. Divide the minutes by 60: 7h 15m = 7.25 hours; 7h 40m = 7.67 hours. Do not write 7h 40m as 7.40, which is only 7h 24m.

Clock times vs durations

This tool adds durations, not times of day. To find how long a shift from 9:30 to 18:15 lasted, subtract 9h 30m from 18h 15m to get 8h 45m. Shifts that cross midnight need 24 hours added to the end time first.

Common mistakes

  • Writing 90 minutes as 1.90 hours instead of 1.5.
  • Entering more than 59 minutes; convert to hours first.

FAQs

How do I convert minutes to decimal hours?

Divide by 60. 45 minutes is 0.75 hours.

Can I add more than two times?

Add two, then use the result as the first duration and add the next.

Can it handle days?

Enter days as hours: 1 day = 24 hours.

Why is my result negative?

The second duration is longer than the first when subtracting.

Does it work with seconds?

No, only hours and minutes.
