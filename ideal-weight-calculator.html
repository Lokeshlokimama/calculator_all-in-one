What this calculator does

Enter your sex and height in centimetres or inches. The calculator returns a single reference weight from the Devine formula. Despite the common name “ideal weight”, this is a historical reference value, not a personal goal.

Formula used

Men: 50 kg + 2.3 kg for every inch over 5 feet

Women: 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg for every inch over 5 feet

Height in centimetres is converted with 1 inch = 2.54 cm. Heights of 5 feet (152.4 cm) or less return the base value.

Worked examples

  • A man 175 cm tall is 68.9 inches, 8.9 inches over 5 feet: 50 + 2.3 × 8.9 = 70.5 kg.
  • A woman 160 cm tall is 63.0 inches, 3.0 inches over 5 feet: 45.5 + 2.3 × 3.0 = 52.4 kg.

What the Devine formula is actually for

Dr Ben Devine published this formula in 1974 to estimate body size for dosing certain medicines. It was never derived from studies of health outcomes, and it gives one number rather than a range. Many people are perfectly healthy well above or below it, and muscular people often weigh more.

For a health-oriented view, a BMI range is more useful. For adults of South Asian origin, Indian guidelines and the WHO suggest a lower healthy BMI cut-off of about 23 rather than 25, because health risks start at lower body weight. For the 175 cm man above, a BMI of 18.5–23 corresponds to about 57–70 kg. Waist size is another useful measure of health risk.

Limitations

Not for children, pregnancy, or heights below 5 feet, where the formula was not designed to be used. It ignores frame size, muscle and age. If you are concerned about your weight, a doctor can assess it properly.

Common mistakes

  • Treating the result as a target to reach.
  • Rounding height to whole inches before calculating, which changes the result by up to about 1 kg.
  • Entering centimetres with the unit set to inches.

FAQs

Is the Devine ideal weight accurate?

It is a consistent reference formula, mainly for medication dosing. It is not a measure of what is healthy for you.

What is a healthy weight range for my height?

Use the BMI calculator. For South Asian adults, a BMI of 18.5 to about 23 is commonly recommended.

Why is the result the same for very short people?

The formula starts at 5 feet; at or below that it returns the base value.

Are there other ideal weight formulas?

Yes, including Robinson, Miller and Hamwi. They give slightly different single values and share the same limitations.

Can I use inches?

Yes. Choose inches from the unit menu.
