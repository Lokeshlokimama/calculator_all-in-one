What this tool does

Fill in who the invoice is from and to, an invoice number and date, an optional due date and notes, and one line per item with a description, quantity and rate. The preview updates as you type and shows the subtotal, discount, tax and total due. When it looks right, download it as a PDF or PNG to send, or as a CSV for your records.

Everything is built in your browser. The invoice details are not uploaded to this site’s server.

How the totals are calculated

Line total = Quantity × Rate

Subtotal = sum of all line totals

Discount = Subtotal × Discount %

Taxable amount = Subtotal − Discount

Tax = Taxable amount × Tax %

Total due = Taxable amount + Tax

The discount is applied before tax, which is the order used on standard GST and VAT invoices.

Worked example

A freelancer bills a client for:

  • Website design, 1 × 25,000 = 25,000
  • Hosting, 12 months × 500 = 6,000

Subtotal 31,000. A 10% discount takes off 3,100, leaving 27,900 taxable. 18% tax on that is 5,022, so the total due is 32,922.

Is this a GST tax invoice?

Not on its own. A GST-registered business in India must show specific details on a tax invoice, including the supplier’s GSTIN, the recipient’s GSTIN for B2B sales, HSN or SAC codes, place of supply, and the tax split into CGST and SGST, or IGST for inter-state supplies. This generator applies one tax percentage and has no separate fields for those.

You can include GSTIN and HSN details in the “From”, “Bill to” and item description fields, but for regular GST billing an accounting or billing tool that produces compliant invoices is the safer choice. This tool suits freelancers, unregistered small businesses, quotes and pro-forma invoices.

Tips for a clear invoice

  • Use a numbering pattern you will keep, such as INV-2026-001, and never reuse a number.
  • Always set a due date and write the payment method in the notes: bank details, UPI ID or a payment link.
  • Describe each line so the client can match it to the work agreed.
  • Check the downloaded PDF before sending it.

Limitations

One tax rate applies to every line, and the discount is a percentage of the subtotal. There is no logo upload, multiple tax rates or saved client list. Data is not saved between visits, so download your invoice before closing the page.

FAQs

Is this invoice generator free?

Yes, with no sign-up and no watermark.

Are my invoice details stored?

No. The invoice is assembled in your browser. The PDF and PNG export libraries are loaded from a public CDN, but your invoice data is not sent to this site.

Can I add GST?

You can add a single tax percentage. A full GST tax invoice needs extra fields such as GSTIN, HSN/SAC and the CGST/SGST or IGST split.

Can I apply a fixed-amount discount?

The discount field takes a percentage, and negative line amounts are not accepted. Convert the amount instead: amount ÷ subtotal × 100. For 1,000 off a 31,000 subtotal, enter 3.2258.

Why does the total show &ldquo;Invalid input&rdquo;?

A quantity, rate, tax or discount is negative or out of range. Tax and discount must be between 0 and 100%.
