What this calculator does

Leave encashment converts unused earned (privilege) leave into money. Enter your monthly basic pay plus dearness allowance (DA) and the number of earned leave days to be encashed. The calculator shows one day’s wage and the total payable.

Formula used

Daily wage = (Basic + DA) ÷ 30

Encashment = Daily wage × Leave days

Dividing by 30 is the basis used for central government employees and many organisations. Some employers divide by 26 working days instead, which gives a higher figure; check your leave policy.

Worked example

Monthly basic plus DA of 45,000 and 20 days of earned leave:

  • Daily wage = 45,000 ÷ 30 = 1,500
  • Encashment = 1,500 × 20 = 30,000

If your employer uses a 26-day basis, the same leave is worth about 34,615. That 4,600 difference is why the divisor in your policy matters.

Tax on leave encashment

Encashment during service is taxed as salary. Encashment at retirement or resignation is treated differently: central and state government employees are fully exempt, while for other employees the exemption is the lowest of several limits, including a lifetime cap set by the government (raised to 25 lakh from April 2023). The calculator shows the gross payout before any tax.

Understanding each input

  • Monthly Basic + DA — the last drawn basic pay plus dearness allowance. Do not use gross salary; HRA and other allowances are normally excluded.
  • Earned leave — the number of encashable days. Many employers limit how many can be carried forward or encashed (for example, 300 days for central government).

Limitations

Your organisation’s leave rules decide the divisor, which pay elements count, the maximum days and whether encashment is allowed during service at all. Under the Factories Act and state Shops and Establishments Acts the rules differ again. Use this as an estimate and confirm with HR or your service rules.

Common mistakes

  • Using gross or CTC instead of basic plus DA.
  • Including casual or sick leave, which usually cannot be encashed.
  • Assuming the whole payout is tax-free for a private-sector job.

FAQs

Is leave encashment calculated on 30 or 26 days?

It depends on your employer. Government rules use 30; some private employers use 26 working days. This calculator uses 30.

Which salary components are included?

Normally basic pay and DA only. Check your leave policy for any other component.

Is leave encashment taxable?

During service, yes, as salary. At retirement, government employees are fully exempt; others get an exemption up to the limits in the Income Tax Act.

Can I encash leave every year?

Only if your employer allows it. Many allow it only at separation.

Does it work for half-days?

Yes. Enter decimals, for example 12.5 days.
