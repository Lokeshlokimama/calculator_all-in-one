What this calculator does

Tap the number and operator buttons to build a calculation, then press = for the answer. C clears everything. The calculator follows the normal order of operations and shows results to up to four decimal places.

Order of operations

Multiplication and division are done before addition and subtraction, working left to right, as taught in school (BODMAS or PEMDAS). So 5 × 4 + 3 = 23, and 3 + 5 × 4 is also 23, not 32. A basic pocket calculator that works strictly left to right would give 32 for the second one; this calculator does not.

Worked examples

  • 12 + 8 × 3 = 12 + 24 = 36
  • 100 ÷ 8 = 12.5
  • 2 ÷ 3 = 0.6667 (rounded to four decimal places)

Everyday shortcuts

  • Percentages: 18% of 2,500 is 2500 × 0.18 = 450. For more percentage tasks, use the percentage calculator.
  • Adding GST: multiply by 1.18 for 18%.
  • Splitting a bill: total ÷ people.
  • Unit price: price ÷ quantity to compare pack sizes, such as 450 ÷ 5 kg = 90 per kg.

Limitations

There are no brackets or memory keys on this simple calculator. For brackets, powers, roots, trigonometry or logarithms, use the scientific calculator. Results are rounded to four decimal places.

FAQs

Does it follow BODMAS?

Yes. Multiplication and division are done before addition and subtraction.

How many decimal places does it show?

Up to four.

Can I use brackets?

Not on this calculator. The scientific calculator supports brackets.

How do I calculate a percentage?

Multiply by the percentage as a decimal, for example × 0.18 for 18%, or use the percentage calculator.

Does it work offline?

Once the page has loaded, calculations run in your browser.
