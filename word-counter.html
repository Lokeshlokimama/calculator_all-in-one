What this tool does

As you type or paste, the tool shows the number of words and the number of characters, including spaces and punctuation. Nothing is sent anywhere; counting happens in your browser.

How words and characters are counted

Words are runs of text separated by spaces, tabs or line breaks. Several spaces in a row still count as one gap. “Hello world” is 2 words; “well-known” is 1 word.

Characters include every letter, digit, space and punctuation mark. “Hello world” is 11 characters.

Typical limits

  • Meta description for search results: around 150–160 characters before it may be cut off.
  • X (Twitter) post: 280 characters on a standard account.
  • SMS: 160 characters for plain English text, fewer for Unicode text such as Hindi.
  • College essays and assignments: usually stated in words; check whether references count.

Counting non-English text

Languages that separate words with spaces, such as Hindi or Tamil, are counted correctly for words. Languages that do not use spaces between words, such as Chinese, Japanese or Thai, will show far fewer words than expected. Character counts use JavaScript string length, so some emoji and a few rare characters count as 2.

FAQs

Does the character count include spaces?

Yes.

Is a hyphenated word one word?

Yes. Only spaces and line breaks separate words.

Why does an emoji count as 2 characters?

Many emoji are stored as two code units in JavaScript strings.

Is my text saved or uploaded?

No. It is counted in your browser only.

Does it count sentences or paragraphs?

No, only words and characters.
